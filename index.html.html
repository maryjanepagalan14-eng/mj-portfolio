About Me

Your Real Estate Operations Partner

I'm Mary Jane, also known as MJ. I help real estate agents and small teams run the back-office side of the business — transaction coordination, CRM management, listing coordination, and social media — so they can stay focused on clients and closings.

At Dana Blanks Real Estate, I managed multi-million-dollar transaction pipelines from contract to close, kept MLS listings and CRM records audit-ready at every stage, and ran the content system across Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn that kept the brand visible between deals. That hands-on real estate experience is exactly what I bring to every client — I already speak the language of contracts, disclosures, and deadlines, so there's no learning curve.

How I Can Help

  • Keep every transaction moving from contract to close — without you chasing signatures
  • Keep your MLS listings accurate and compliant before they ever go live
  • Keep your CRM and client records clean, accurate, and audit-ready
  • Keep your content calendar running so your socials never go quiet between deals
