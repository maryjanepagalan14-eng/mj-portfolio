02

Why This Format

"Composed, not chatty — precise, not padded."

  1. Subject line does the work. Property + week + status — an agent can act on the subject line alone without opening the email.
  2. Leads with the headline, not a greeting essay. "Everything's moving as planned" up front, details after.
  3. Checkmarks over paragraphs. Scannable in five seconds from a phone between showings.
  4. Always states what's needed from them. Even when it's "nothing" — removes the guesswork of whether to check in.
  5. Same day, every week. Predictability is the actual value — they stop needing to ask "any updates?" because they already know when it's coming.
